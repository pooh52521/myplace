Finora Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Finora ("the App") is an Android application for personal finance management, transaction tracking, and spending analysis. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, and what choices users have.

Transaction, goal, and profile data is stored on the user's device by default. The App does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs. If a user chooses to use AI features, limited information may be processed through the OpenRouter API.

2. SMS Permission and Processing

If the user grants SMS receive permission, the App checks newly received messages for financial or relevant life-event information. The App first processes messages on the device using keywords and regular expressions. Messages identified as one-time passwords or verification codes are excluded from transaction processing.

Possible transactions are presented for review and may be saved or rejected by the user. SMS permission can be revoked at any time in Android settings. The App does not use SMS for advertising, marketing, or sale.

3. AI and Third-Party Services

When a user uses an AI feature, or when local processing cannot determine transaction details, the App may send a limited request to the OpenRouter API. The request may contain the user's question or limited content from the message being analyzed.

Before AI-assisted SMS analysis, the App limits the text to 500 characters and masks sequences of six or more consecutive digits. Masking cannot guarantee that all identifying information is removed. Users should not submit sensitive messages or questions to the AI feature.

Processing by OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider may be subject to their own terms and privacy policies. The user supplies the OpenRouter API key. The App stores it locally in Android DataStore and does not send it to the App developer's server.

4. Storage and Security

  • Transactions, goals, challenges, SMS notifications, and profile data are stored locally in a Room database.
  • App settings and the API key are stored locally using Android DataStore.
  • The app-lock PIN is stored as a hash, not as plain text.
  • CSV exports are temporarily created in the App cache and can be shared through an app selected by the user.
  • Android device backup behavior depends on the operating system and the user's device settings. The App cannot protect against loss, rooting, or compromise of the device itself.

6. Sharing and Sale

The App does not sell personal information. When the user invokes an AI feature, necessary information may be sent to OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider to process that request. Except where required by law, the App does not provide personal information to other third parties.

7. Retention and Deletion

The App does not maintain a separate developer server containing the user's financial data. Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it or uninstalls the App. Data included in Android backups may remain according to the operating system's backup policy and may need to be deleted separately from device settings.

Users can delete transactions and app data using the App's deletion features or by uninstalling the App. The stored OpenRouter API key can be removed from the API key settings.

8. Children's Privacy
