Finora Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 9, 2026

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Finora account and associated data, email srdjandev0@gmail.com using the subject Finora account and data deletion.

In your request, include the email address used in the app, if any, and explain that you want your Finora account and associated data deleted. Do not include passwords, API keys, card numbers, or bank account numbers.

Data deleted

  • Local user profile and account identifier
  • Transactions, SMS-derived transaction records, and life-item records
  • Savings goals and spending challenges
  • App profile settings and stored API key

Data retained

Finora does not maintain a developer-hosted account database. Data stored only on your device is deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Data contained in Android device backups may require separate deletion through your device or Google backup settings.

We may retain the deletion request itself and related correspondence for up to 30 days to verify and process the request, after which it is deleted unless a longer period is required by law.

Processing time: We normally process deletion requests within 30 days. If we need clarification to identify the request, we may contact you at the email address provided.

For more information, see the Finora Privacy Policy.
